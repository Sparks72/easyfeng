EasyFeng · repeaters made easy for Baofeng radios

1Choose your radio, connect and read it

  1. Switch the radio off. Plug the cable into the radio (fold the rubber cover right back and push firmly) and into the PC.
  2. Switch the radio on and turn the volume up a little.
  3. Press Read radio and choose the cable's COM port. A backup of the radio is saved automatically.
Not connected

2Pick repeaters and stations

Distances are from .

Sources: repeatermap.de list (Dec 2022), RepeaterBook, afu-nord.de and the 2019 DL5SAF list. Each card says how current its entry is. Not every repeater uses a CTCSS tone: where a card shows one, the channel sends it. Where none is listed, the repeater opens on carrier or with the 1750 Hz burst (step 3). If one doesn't open, edit its card and add the tone. Airband channels are receive only (the radio cannot transmit on them) and use AM. Airband frequencies come from OurAirports and the Fehmarn airfield site; check them against a current chart.

3Choose channels and check

With the tone burst at 1750 Hz, hold PTT and press the upper side key to open a repeater that needs it.

4Write to the radio

Only the chosen channels are changed. Everything else in the radio stays as it is.

5Put a backup back

Restores the whole radio from a backup this page saved, or from a CHIRP .img file of the same model.
